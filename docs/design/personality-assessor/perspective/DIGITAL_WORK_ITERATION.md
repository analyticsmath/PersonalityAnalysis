## Timing and input

One GSAP clock drives Lenis. Scroll smoothing shortened to .85 seconds and scene scrubs to .3–.35 seconds. Fixed photograph dimensions mean lazy loads need no page refresh. No vertical wheel interception. Desktop conveyor repeats three measured identical cycles, wraps in both directions, damps drag momentum, pauses on hover/focus and has an explicit play/pause button. Offscreen and hidden-document motion pauses. Touch/reduced-motion use native horizontal scrolling and a single accessible sequence. Duplicate links are excluded from keyboard and accessibility trees.
